Portal Support & Guidelines

Resources and guidelines for utilizing the Business Intelligence Portal effectively.

Dashboard Usage Guidelines

The Power BI Dashboard embedded within this portal is refreshed daily at 02:00 AM EST. Any changes made via the Manage Data tab by administrators will reflect in the subsequent scheduled refresh cycle, ensuring high performance during operational hours.

Roles & Permissions

  • Standard Users: Can view the Dashboard, review Key Insights, and download PDF reports.
  • Administrators: Have full access, including the ability to add, edit, or delete inventory and sales records directly from the portal.
